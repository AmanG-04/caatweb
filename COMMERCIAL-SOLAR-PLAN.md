# Commercial Solar Website & SEO Plan

## Goal
Position CAAT PowerBot as a commercial-first solar EPC company in Delhi NCR.
Lead with electricity-cost reduction, engineering and completed projects.
Assess economics without assuming residential subsidy. Keep residential services secondary if desired.

## Website Changes
- Homepage: commercial headline, rooftop/project imagery, assessment CTA and verified commercial references.
- Suggested headline: “Commercial Rooftop Solar in Delhi NCR.”
- Main CTA: “Request a Solar Assessment.”
- Homepage order: hero, projects, benefits, feasibility, delivery process, related solutions, FAQs, enquiry.
- Remove residential subsidy tables from the main journey.
- `/solutions/solar`: replace home-sizing content with daytime usage, roof assessment, connection constraints, installation scope, savings assumptions and AMC.
- Process: bill/load review, roof assessment, design, applicable approvals, installation and commissioning.
- FAQs: commercial savings, approvals, roof requirements, downtime, warranties and maintenance.
- Contact/footer: commercial-first wording and contextual enquiries.
- Retain the existing visual design and shared components.

## Project Proof
- Prioritise healthcare, education and business projects already documented.
- Relabel “Testimonials” as “Projects” or “Case Studies”; retain the URL initially.
- Case studies: requirement, verified capacity, scope, location, photos and measured outcomes where available.
- Distinguish PV solar, BESS, solar water heating and electrical work.
- Resolve inconsistent capacities, equipment details and units before publication.
- Avoid unsupported industrial experience or guaranteed payback claims.

## Lead Journey & Calculator
- Collect company, contact, sector, city, monthly usage/bill, roof permission and timeline.
- Follow up with sanctioned load, daytime usage, roof area, existing DG/BESS and bills.
- Allow unknown technical inputs.
- Current calculator is subsidy-independent but estimates generation/savings only.
- Remove CTA promises of subsidy, investment or payback.
- Label results preliminary.
- Later: versioned commercial model covering self-consumption, export valuation, roof/load limits, CAPEX and O&M.
- Preserve existing API, validation, storage and historical quote contracts.

## SEO
- Homepage focus: commercial solar company / solar EPC in Delhi NCR.
- Primary solar page: `/solutions/solar`.
- Redirect `/commercial-solar` directly to `/solutions/solar`.
- Improve existing city pages with commercial content and genuine local evidence.
- Add healthcare/education pages when sufficient unique proof exists.
- Align titles, H1s, descriptions, canonicals, internal links and schema.
- Add article metadata, breadcrumbs and explicit noindex for personalised quote results.
- Check sitemap dates, canonical-domain consistency and blog-image crawlability.
- Review traffic/backlinks before retiring residential or subsidy URLs.
- Validate keyword demand and rankings before expanding pages.

## Priority Content
1. Commercial solar without subsidy: investment assessment.
2. Which commercial electricity charges can solar reduce?
3. EPC quotation inclusions and exclusions.
4. Solar for hospitals and colleges.
5. Commercial approvals by verified jurisdiction.
6. Solar, batteries and DG integration.

Improve blog headings, tables, internal links and expert attribution first.

## Rollout
1. Positioning: confirm sectors/project size, verify facts, update homepage, solar page, CTAs, FAQs and metadata.
2. Conversion: qualification fields, case studies, regional/sector pages and attribution.
3. Growth: commercial financial model, improved publishing and targeted content.

## Success Metrics
Qualified commercial leads, assessments, proposals, win rate and won capacity/value.

## Decisions Required
- Commercial-first or commercial-only?
- Minimum preferred project size and priority sectors?
- EPC only, or financing/RESCO/PPA too?
